ABOUT US

We are Leading in Coco Coir Technology.

At M Coir , We are manufacturer, exporter and supplier of Coconut Fiber, Coco Peat and Coir based products. The varied range of products that we make from the fiber. Coir is popular all over the world for their eco- friendly attributes and durability.

As our Coir and Horticulture products are made out of the finest and pure coir from Sri Lanka which is one of the best sources for coir and coco peat in the world. If you are looking for really good quality Coir and Coco Peat products we at M COIR will be most as welcome to provide you with products from our range.

We market a wide range of high Quality coconut fibre & Horticulture products ranging from Growing media and horticulture products, Bedding & Upholstery, Industrial Fiber such as Coir Twine, Twisted Fiber, Mattress/Mixed Fiber Bales and Erosion Control products.

We have also been most sincere to our esteemed customers over the years in catering to all their order and specifications executing each order in time at the most competitive prices.
